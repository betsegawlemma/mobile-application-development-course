## Privacy policy

Write in plain words, for a user, not for a lawyer.

1. **Who we are.** The app, the group, and an email address for
   questions.
2. **What the app collects.** Each kind of data, in the user's words
   (for example "your email address", "a photo you choose to attach").
3. **Why.** One reason for each kind of data.
4. **Where it is stored, and who can see it.** On the phone only, on
   your server, or with a service such as Firebase. Who else can read it
   (other users, nobody).
5. **What leaves the phone without being stored.** For example text sent
   to an AI service.
6. **How to delete it.** In the app, or by email; how long it takes.
7. **Changes.** How users learn about a new version of this policy.
