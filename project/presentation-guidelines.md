# Project presentation

**When:** week 15 · **Length:** 12 minutes plus 5 minutes of questions ·
**Weight:** 15 % of the project mark

## Format

1. **Problem and users** — who has the problem, why an app.
2. **Live demonstration** — on a real phone if possible, mirrored to the
   projector (use `scrcpy` or the emulator as a backup). Show the main
   user stories end to end, including one error case (no network, invalid
   input).
3. **Architecture** — one diagram: features, layers, data sources. Point
   at one piece of code you are proud of and one you would change.
4. **Testing and process** — what is tested, how CI runs, how the group
   worked.
5. **Reflection** — what you learned; what you would do differently; how
   you used AI tools and what you verified.

Every member speaks. Prepare for the questions; anyone may be asked about
any part of the code.

## Marking (15 points)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Demonstration works and shows the main stories | 5 |
| Clear explanation of architecture and data flow | 4 |
| Testing and process explained with evidence (CI, coverage) | 2 |
| Honest reflection including AI use | 2 |
| Answers to questions — **scored per member** | 2 |

Each member is asked at least one question about code and scores 0
(cannot answer), 1 (partly) or 2 (correct and complete). That score is
your own; the other 13 points are shared by the group. Before the
presentation, submit `USER_TEST.md` (see `milestones.md`): the examiners
may ask about what your test user found.

## Practical advice

- Rehearse with a timer. Twelve minutes is shorter than you expect.
- Have the app installed and opened before you start; do not sign in live
  with a slow network — prepare a signed-in state.
- If the demonstration fails, have a short screen recording ready.
- Slides: at most eight, large text, no code longer than ten lines.
